# ---- Cuts of meat cooked whole. A recipe of one ingredient named with one of these is a Simple dish ("Elk Steak"), not a
# preparation step, unless the name also has an UNCOOKED word ("Raw Bear Steak" is only cut). They don't set a tier.
CUT	steak, haunch, chop, chops, cutlet
UNCOOKED	raw, uncooked
# ---- Baked foods: made only at an oven, like our pies and breads, and they keep their flour. BAKED words count only as the
# ---- last word of the name ("Walnut Bread" is baked, "Bread Soup" is a soup), not counting a SERVING word after it
# ---- ("Bread Roll Basket"), and also at the end of a longer word ("cupcake", "cornbread"); BAKING words count anywhere.
